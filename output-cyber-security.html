Narrative report

On August 15, 2026, I took part in the one-hour professional development webinar “Cyber Awareness: Digital Identity | Protect Yourself Online,” organized and conducted by UpskillTechPH Training Services. The session was delivered by resource speaker Vherbik Troy Cardenas together with founder and program lead Alexander Lopez. It gave me a structured way to look at my own cybersecurity, how I manage my presence online, and how to protect my accounts against modern threats.

The discussion showed how digital identities are built, exposed and exploited across platforms. My biggest takeaway was the need for proactive digital hygiene: every action online adds to an identifiable footprint that can be used for phishing, identity theft and credential harvesting. Staying safe is a continuous practice of personal vigilance, deliberate data-sharing habits and strong defenses, not something to leave to automated security tools.

The same ideas show up in industry practice: Zero Trust (“never trust, always verify”), multi-factor authentication, identity and access management, single sign-on, and AI-assisted monitoring that flags suspicious logins in real time. In IT work they translate into strict access control, passwordless sign-in and regular security audits; in the community, into workshops and simple online-safety guides for students and small businesses.

Key takeaways

  • Every action online adds to a digital footprint that attackers can use for phishing, identity theft and credential harvesting.
  • Protecting your identity is an ongoing habit: share deliberately, verify before you trust, and turn on multi-factor authentication.
  • Organizations apply the same thinking at scale through Zero Trust, identity and access management, and regular security audits.
